# Micro-betting market landscape for a vision-driven event emitter

Research agent report, 2026-10-31. Web research only (WebSearch/WebFetch; bet365 and several bookmaker help centres block fetching, so those are covered from search-engine snippets of the help pages plus secondary sources, flagged below). Two PDFs were extracted locally with pdftotext: DraftKings house rules (8.18.25) and the StatsBomb Open Data Events v4.0.0 spec.

## Headline findings

1. The micro-markets that actually trade are overwhelmingly **"next X"** on a discrete, officially-scored unit: next pitch (ball/strike/in play, speed, fastball), next plate appearance result, next NFL play (pass/rush, yards, first down, TD), next drive outcome, next NBA possession result / next field-goal type, next tennis point/serve outcome. Sportradar's UOF publishes the exact market IDs nVenue and Huddle distribute (lists below).
2. **"First X" player markets are shrinking**, not growing. On 16 Oct 2026 the NBA told sportsbooks they can no longer offer bets on who scores the first basket, first 3, first rebound/assist/steal/block, whether a player misses his first/next shot, or who wins the opening tip ([news4jax, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.news4jax.com/sports/2026/10/16/some-wagers-certain-parlays-no-longer-can-be-offered-to-bettors-nba-tells-sportsbooks/)). MLB capped individual-pitch bets (ball/strike, velocity) at $200 and barred them from parlays after the Clase/Ortiz indictment ([SI, 10 Nov 2025](https://www.si.com/mlb/mlb-sportsbooks-agree-to-set-wager-limit-on-individual-pitch-bets)); Ohio is drafting a rule removing player-specific in-game micro props after the "first pitch" investigation ([ESPN, 8 Aug 2025](https://www.espn.com/espn/betting/story/_/id/45927585/ohio-regulators-weighing-ban-first-pitch-wagers-other-prop-bets)). Team-level "first team to score", "first corner", "first throw-in" are unaffected.
3. Every provider settles on the **official league stat feed** (Genius for NFL, Sportradar for NBA/MLB, Opta for soccer props), not video. DraftKings: bets "are settled using the statistics provided by the Event's official statistical provider or the Event's official governing body" (house rules §II). So a vision system's output must map 1:1 onto those feeds' event vocabularies; the value is latency/redundancy, not being the settlement source.
4. No provider publishes a measured end-to-end latency. nVenue claims "<50ms prediction generation" and "sub-second" delivery; Genius BetVision's stream runs 4–6 s behind live (about 30–40 s ahead of TV); Kero says new markets every ~30 s; Huddle resulting is "completely automated" off play-by-play.

## 4. Settlement definitions (quotes)

**Official-source rule.** DraftKings: "bets are settled using the statistics provided by the Event's official statistical provider or the Event's official governing body" ([DK house rules 8.18.25, MGC PDF](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/DraftKings-House-Rules-8.18.25.pdf)). Sky Bet NFL: "All bets will be settled as per the sport's official website, NFL.com" ([skybet](https://support.skybet.com/app/answers/detail/american-football-rules/)). bet365 soccer props settle on Opta ([bet365 Opta FAQ](https://help.bet365.com/s/en-gb/sports/how-is-my-bet-settled)).

**Soccer (Opta/Stats Perform definitions, [theanalyst.com](https://theanalyst.com/articles/opta-football-stats-definitions); statsperform.com returns 403):**
- Shot on Target: "A deliberate attempt to score that is on target." DraftKings reproduces the full Opta text: includes goals and keeper saves, "also includes shots on target that are blocked by a last line defending player"; a shot hitting the frame "is classified as a Shot Off Target unless the ball subsequently enters the net"; "A blocked shot is not classified as a Shot Off Target" (DK PDF, soccer player props). bet365: "Shots include all Shots on Target, all Shots off Target and all Blocked Shots".
- Key Pass: "The final pass from a player to their teammate who then attempts a shot on goal without scoring." Assist: "The final touch from a teammate that leads to the recipient of the ball scoring a goal."
- Tackle: "connects with the ball in a legal, ground-level challenge." Touch: "A sum of all collected events where a player touches the ball." Interception: "reads an opponent's pass and intercepts the ball by moving into the line of the intended pass." Corner: "When the ball has left the field of play for a corner." Possession: "one or more sequences in a row belonging to the same team." Big Chance: "a situation where a player should reasonably be expected to score."
- First corner / throw-in (LSports rulebook, [docs.lsports.eu football](https://docs.lsports.eu/u/trade/settlements-rulebook/football.md)): "First Match Corner - 12 (ID 998)" settles on the first team *awarded* a corner; no corners → both lose; "First Throw-in in the Match (ID 1849)" on the team awarded the first throw-in; "No throw-in data received → Not Set". bet365 corners: "corners awarded but not taken do not count"; time-of-first-corner = time taken, not awarded ([bet365 corner rules](https://help.bet365.com/s/en/sportsrules/soccer/corner-markets)).
- Next goal / goalscorer (DK): "Next Goal Markets … bets on the 'Neither' Selection will be settled as won" if no further goal; own goals excluded: "the next non-own goal … will be considered the 1st or next goal". bet365 Race to X corners: "settled on the team who reach the quoted number of corners first".

**Basketball:**
- First basket: bet365 player markets "Free Throws do count for settlement purposes", starter required, "Exact" variant uses "the exact method of score as detailed in the official game play-by-play" ([bet365](https://help.bet365.com/s/en/sportsrules/basketball)); bet365 game bets "Free Throws do not count" for next-FG style markets; FanDuel counts FTs, BetMGM "First Field Goal" excludes them, DK rules say FTs count but bet slip says no ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/do-free-throws-count-for-first-basket-scored-props)). Now banned league-wide for US books (NBA memo 16 Oct 2026).
- Next possession (DK): "Possession Result Exact … settled in accordance with the 1st outcome"; "Team possession ends with a score, after all free throw attempt(s), if any, or when the opposing team gains possession"; period end before a "valid shot attempt, turnover, or free throws" voids; technical-foul FT counts as "Attempted Free Throw"/"Score".
- Race to X (Paddy Power): "Resulted on the team to achieve the specified total in the specified period first"; OT excluded ([paddypower](https://helpcenter.paddypower.com/app/answers/detail/basketball-rules/)); DK: "If 'No Player' or 'No Team' is not offered … bets are settled as lost."
- Tip-off: no operator rule text found anywhere; Hard Rock possession rule: "A jump ball in which the defensive team comes up with the possession will settle as a turnover" ([Hard Rock Cincinnati PDF](https://casino.hardrock.com/cincinnati/-/media/project/shrss/hri/casinos/hard-rock/cincinnati/sportsbook/rules-and-forms/sb-houserules-2024.pdf?rev=e08195cc6297405c8bb949d19e111f2d)). Sportradar's feed carries `opentip` "with who gains possession". NBA has now banned tip-off wagers.

**NFL:**
- Touchdown scorer (DK): "the player in possession of the football in the opposing team's end zone"; passer never counts; unlisted player or penalty TD → all lose. Sky Bet: no TD → first/last TD scorer void; coin toss settles on "the official on-field coin toss … If the toss is repeated, the final toss counts."
- Next play (DK): void "if a play does not occur due to the end of the half or Game"; "A sack will be settled as a pass attempt for NFL Games" (rush for NCAA); TD = first down "Yes"; accepted post-snap penalty negating the play voids. bet365: pre-snap penalty bets stand; nullified play void; play-stands-with-penalty settles on pre-penalty official stats.
- Drive (bet365): must cross the stated yard line; "void in the event of an incomplete drive, unless the outcome is already determined"; new set of downs by run, pass or automatic-first-down penalty only.
- First team to score: DK "1st To Score/Moneyline" = "scores the 1st point in the Game"; "Coin Toss Winner … opening coin toss of the Game only."

**MLB (DK Pitch Markets):** "Hit by pitch and a pitch out are considered a ball"; "In Play" wins on anything except "a ball, hit by pitch, pitch out, strike, or foul"; "A dropped 3rd strike is considered a strike"; intentional ball/walk void; "A pitch timer violation does not count as a pitch". Betr: "Pitch Result … Void if the pitch isn't completed." Unnamed operator: "Strike" only swinging/called ([intercom help](https://intercom.help/support-help-center/en/articles/12007153-baseball)). Betcris at-bat grouping: fielder's choice = other; HBP = other; HR = hit ([betcris](https://ayuda.betcris.com/en/learn-to-bet/live-betting/baseball-next-play-betting-rules/)). No operator defines "first pitch strike" separately; it is the generic pitch-result market applied to pitch 1.

**Tennis:** bet365 Point Betting void if the wrong server is shown; "If a point isn't played because the game or match ends, all bets on that point will be void"; next-game markets no action if the next game is a tiebreak except "Next Game First Point" ([bet365 tennis](https://help.bet365.com/s/en-us/sportsrules/tennis)). Ace (ATP): "a serve that lands inside the service box and goes untouched by the receiving player" ([atptour.com](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/tennis-explained-learn-the-game)). DK table tennis: point/game awarded as an umpire penalty → void.

## 6. Per-sport candidate markets, settlement, encoding, video difficulty

Difficulty is from a single broadcast feed with no tracking data; "easy" = one visible, unambiguous physical event; "medium" = needs player ID, an official's decision, or sub-second timing; "hard" = depends on a judgement the official scorer makes (intent, "on target", awarded-vs-taken) or on off-camera information.

### Soccer

| Market | Settlement definition | Standard encoding | Video difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Next goal / xth goal (UOF 8) | DK: no further goal → "Neither"; own goals count for team markets, not goalscorer | SB Shot outcome 97 Goal; SR score_change; Live Data 30 (+1018 possible_goal) | Easy: ball over line + celebration/restart; VAR reversals are the risk (SR has 1019 Goal canceled) |
| Next/first team to score | as above | SR `score_change`; UOF 30 "Which team to score" | Easy |
| First/next goalscorer (UOF 38/892) | DK: own goals excluded; non-starters void | SB Shot.player; SR players[type=scorer] | Medium: player ID on the scorer, deflections re-attribute |
| First/next corner (LSports 998, UOF 163) | LSports: team *awarded* the corner; bet365 total/race markets count only corners *taken* | SR corner_kick / Live Data 154 (side), 1016 possible corner; SB Pass.type 61 Corner | Easy-medium: awarded = referee signal (often off-frame); taken = kick from the arc (easy) |
| Race to X corners (UOF 601) | bet365: first to reach quoted number | count of taken corners | Easy once corner-taken detection works |
| First/next throw-in (LSports 1849) | team awarded; "no throw-ins" → both lose | SR throw_in / 152; SB Pass.type 67 | Easy: ball out over touchline + restart; team attribution easy |
| Next goal kick / free kick / offside | LSports "Not Set" if data missing | SR goal_kick/free_kick/offside (151/150/153); SB Pass.type 62/63, Offside 8 | Medium: free kick and offside are referee decisions; goal kick easy |
| First/next shot (team or player) (SportingRisk 1634/1641) | Opta: "deliberate attempt to score" (intent) | SB Shot (16); SR shot_on_target+shot_off_target+shot_blocked; Opta Shot | Medium: distinguishing a shot from a miscued cross is a scorer judgement |
| First/next shot on target (1635/1642) | Opta: on-target incl. last-line blocks; woodwork off target unless it goes in; keeper save counts | SB outcome ∈ {Goal, Saved, Saved To Post}; SR shot_on_target / 155 | Hard: needs trajectory-to-frame projection, last-line-block rule, and camera angle |
| Next booking / first card (UOF 137) | referee card shown | SR yellow_card/red_card; Live Data 40/45/50, 1084 possible yellow | Easy-medium: card is visible; player ID medium |
| Player "first touch" / next pass / key pass | Opta Touch and Key Pass definitions; no live market found | SB Ball Receipt 42, Pass 30, Carry 43; Opta Touch | Hard for settlement value (no market); easy-medium technically |
| Goal in next 5/10/15 min (UOF 102–110) | official clock | SR match_time/stoppage_time | Easy (clock OCR) |
| Time of first goal/corner | bet365: time corner *taken* | SR match_clock | Easy |

## 7. What could not be verified

- bet365's exact in-play menu labels for basketball tip-off, baseball "Next Pitch", tennis ace markets, and NFL first-TD-scorer rules (site and help centre block automated access; use a human browser session).
- Pinnacle's rules page: `pinnacle.com/en/betting-rules` returns 301→404 and no help-centre page surfaced in search.
- Sportradar UOF basketball/tennis market lists render client-side (pages fetch empty); ATP micro market IDs are only in the authenticated `markets.xml`/Player Markets resource.
- Kaggle Big Data Bowl data dictionaries render client-side; the event list above is from the CMU workshop (2021-season weeks 1–8) plus a 2025 paper, so 2025 values may differ (e.g. `tackle`, `out_of_bounds` not in the CMU table).
- Statcast `description` enumeration is not published on the CSV docs page; values quoted are those observed in public Statcast exports (e.g. [Hugging Face statcast-era-pitches](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jensen-holm/statcast-era-pitches)).
- No provider publishes a settlement rule for "tip-off winner", "first shot", or "first serve ace" beyond deferring to official data; LSports is the only rulebook with explicit "first corner/throw-in" logic.
